why this exists
There is a specific vertigo induced by anyone standing at a San Francisco intersection who travels along the third axis of memory — the realization that the ground itself is the only continuous character in the whole story… that the cove was filled, and the ships were buried, and the hotels burned, and the parking lots (there were always, at some point, parking lots) got towers poured over them, and through every one of these reversals the parcel of land just sat there, keeping its shape, holding its officially registered number, accumulating occupants the way a page accumulates handwriting. This project is an attempt to read through the written drafts: 4,619 geocoded photographs, each tied back to its archive because the whole point is that nobody no longer needs to blindly take anyone’s word for what stood where. Peel through the layers of the city to find what once was.
